TX–84 / QUICK START GUIDE

Welcome aboard,
space cabbie.

All 24 classic levels, rebuilt for a new orbit. Deliver each fare, then take the final passenger through the exit when they say “Up, please!”

01

Feel the gravity.

Hold W or ↑ to rise. Use A / D to steer. Some levels reverse gravity or controls: check the level hint.

02

Stick the landing.

SPACE toggles landing gear. Deployed gear disables both side thrusters. Retract it to steer; line up and brake before deploying it to land. Descend below 3 m/s. Match a moving pad’s speed before touching down.

03

Learn the route.

Fly into portal rings to teleport. Touch diamond switches to open doors. Wait for gaps in electrical bands and for lasers to go dark.

04

Go the distance.

Refuel at Pad F or a marked depot. Every fourth classic level restores a taxi, up to three. Progress saves at the start of each level.
